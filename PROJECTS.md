## Customer success & support

- **[The Support Machine](https://assafbar2.github.io/the-support-machine/)**. A free field guide to operating AI customer support. The Second Edition adds six chapters on organizational resistance, build versus buy, customer agents, action receipts, recovery, and continuing evaluation. 29 chapters, 10 illustrations, 80 prompts, two AI skills. [Read online](https://assafbar2.github.io/the-support-machine/book.html) · [PDF](https://assafbar2.github.io/the-support-machine/the-support-machine-second-edition.pdf) · [Edition notes](https://assafbar2.github.io/the-support-machine/notes.html) · [Install the skill](https://assafbar2.github.io/the-support-machine/skill.html) · [Source](https://github.com/assafbar2/the-support-machine) · [Cover artwork](https://assafbar2.github.io/the-support-machine/assets/second-edition-social.png).
- **[Promise Ledger](https://github.com/assafbar2/promise-ledger)**. Check whether a feature promised to a customer is actually available on their account. [Demo](https://promise-ledger-chi.vercel.app/app).
- **[RECEIPT](https://github.com/assafbar2/receipt)**. A support agent crashed after attempting an action. Check what happened before retrying.
- **[Stage Manager (CALL-E)](https://github.com/assafbar2/customer-success-voice-signal-hackathon)**. When an account needs a decision, call the person who owns it, not the customer.
- **[fcli](https://github.com/assafbar2/fcli)**. Test Fin answers and check urgent Intercom tickets from the terminal.
- **[barnirhired](https://barnirhired.com/)**. A job-fit tool for people looking for technical, customer-facing roles.

## AI & developer tools

- **[Agent Switchboard](https://agentswitchboard.dev)**. A directory of 400+ agents for humans and agents, with MCP and webMCP. [Source](https://github.com/assafbar2/agentswitchboard.dev).
- **[Suspect Memories](https://github.com/assafbar2/suspect-memories)**. A hackathon prototype that traces an agent’s regression to the memory that caused it.
- **[Greenroom](https://github.com/assafbar2/greenroom)**. Ask a panel of models to debate a question before you choose an answer.
- **[Ground Truth](https://github.com/assafbar2/ground-truth)**. Compare five AI-written editorial perspectives on the same news topic.
- **[Flux](https://github.com/assafbar2/flux-hackathon-2026)**. A hackathon build that helps a new hire find who owns what and which GitLab issue to take first.
- **[Sentry Playground](https://github.com/assafbar2/Sentry-Playground)**. Trigger errors in an instrumented React app and see what Sentry captures.
- **[Muse connector (unofficial)](https://github.com/assafbar2/unofficial-sentry-muse-connector)**. An independent sketch of a Sentry connector for Meta Muse.

## Games & simulations

- **[Rumor Room](https://assafbar2.github.io/Rumor-Room-Muse-Edition/)**. Ten fictional scandals, four research turns, one lie. [Case study](https://www.barnir.co/builds/rumor-room) · [Original AI build](https://github.com/assafbar2/rumor-room#readme).
- **[Conflict: 2027](https://conflict-2027.vercel.app/)**. A hackathon-built political simulation: can you negotiate a peace that lasts 48 turns? Playable; source private.
- **[Prime Suspects](https://assafbar2.github.io/prime-suspects/)**. Twelve numbers in a lineup, one culprit, five probes.
- **[Odometer](https://odometer-game.vercel.app)**. A number puzzle. Playable; source private.
