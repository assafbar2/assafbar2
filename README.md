## Selected work

### The Support Machine

I wrote this free field guide for the CX leaders who have to sell AI experience and support to the executive team and earn the trust of the people who own the queue. I cover the AI NIMBY problem (“yes, but here?”) and how to recognize the skeptic who’s right to stop the launch.

The playbook, the science, quality and correctness evaluations, humans in the loop (sorry), the new roles in the team, real KPIs, and more.

[Read the Second Edition](https://assafbar2.github.io/the-support-machine/book.html) · [Install the AI skill and chat with the book](https://assafbar2.github.io/the-support-machine/skill.html)

### Promise Ledger

Engineering closed the ticket. The customer still can’t see the button. I built Promise Ledger to catch that gap before an account team tells a customer, “It’s live.”

[Try the demo](https://promise-ledger-chi.vercel.app/app)

### Mainframe Lab

Write IBM assembly in your browser. Run one instruction, watch a register change, then step backward to see exactly what it did.

[Open the lab](https://assafbar2.github.io/mainframe-assembly-lab/)
